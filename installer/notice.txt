Magical Blocks - unofficial fan project

This setup does two things:
  1. installs the game program and its shaders,
  2. lets you pick your own ROM file of the game, checks it, and saves it for the game.

You must supply your own legally dumped ROM of Magical Tetris Challenge (USA, Nintendo 64).
We do not provide it, and this program does not contain the game's graphics, music or sound.
